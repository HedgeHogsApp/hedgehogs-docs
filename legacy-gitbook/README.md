# What is Hedgehog Protocol?

**Hedgehog Protocol** is a next-generation DeFi automation platform that enables anyone to access institutional-grade position management across Aave, Uniswap V3, and beyond.

Following the discovery that most DeFi users lose money to approval fees, manual management, and missed opportunities, we built a solution that fundamentally changes the economics of DeFi participation. We're revolutionizing how people interact with decentralized finance—making sophisticated strategies accessible, affordable, and automated for everyone.

## Why Hedgehog?

Hedgehog is our answer to the DeFi profitability gap. It's a platform where efficiency, automation, and self-custody come together. Traditional DeFi forces users to pay $150-200 in approval fees before making their first dollar, then requires constant monitoring to avoid losses. Meanwhile, institutions run automated systems that capture 2-3x better yields with the same capital.

With Hedgehog, we level the playing field. Our proxy-centric architecture reduces approval costs by 95%, our automation network monitors positions every block, and our connector system enables complex multi-protocol strategies that were previously only accessible to sophisticated players. From just-in-time approvals to automated rebalancing and cross-protocol orchestration, Hedgehog is rethinking how DeFi should work—simple, efficient, and built for users.

## DeFi Automation is a Multi-Billion Dollar Opportunity

**The automation gap costs users billions annually:** Every day, retail DeFi users lose 30-60% of potential yields to manual management inefficiencies. Uniswap V3 positions sit out of range earning zero fees. Rewards go unclaimed losing compound interest. Health factors drop unnoticed leading to liquidations. Collectively, this represents billions in lost value that automation could capture.

**Hedgehog is uniquely positioned:** We've solved the core technical challenge—enabling automation across protocols that don't support delegation (like Uniswap V3). We're positioned to capture a significant portion of this multi-billion dollar opportunity.

## New Platform, Clear Focus

Hedgehog is laser-focused on building the best DeFi automation infrastructure in the space. We're committed to delivering a seamless, powerful, and truly decentralized position management experience.

### Stage 1: Core Automation (Live)

**Delve into Hedgehog**

Hedgehog introduces three core automation capabilities:

**Position Automation:** Set-and-forget management for Aave V3 lending/borrowing and Uniswap V3 liquidity positions. AutoRebalance keeps your LPs in range, AutoCompound reinvests fees, AutoRepay prevents liquidation—all executing automatically with lower fees than manual management.

**Cross-Protocol Strategies:** Advanced strategies that coordinate between multiple DeFi protocols. Leveraged yield farming (borrow on Aave, LP on Uniswap), delta-neutral positions (earn fees without price risk), recursive lending (maximize APY through automated loops)—institutional strategies now accessible to everyone.

**JIT Approval System:** Approve your personal smart contract once per token, never pay protocol approval fees again. Your proxy handles all downstream approvals automatically, reducing setup costs from $150-200 to $6-10.

We continue to expand protocol support beyond Aave V3 and Uniswap V3, with connectors for Compound, Curve, and Balancer in development.

### Stage 2: Advanced Automation (Q2 2025)

- **Strategy Marketplace:** Community-built automation strategies with revenue sharing

### Stage 3: The Future of DeFi Automation

Our long-term vision includes intent-based automation that simplifies DeFi by automatically executing optimal strategies across chains and protocols, AI-powered strategy optimization, and a fully decentralized bot network that ensures censorship-resistant automation.
