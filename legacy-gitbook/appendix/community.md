## Community Governance (Future)

### Governance Process

**Proposal lifecycle:**
1. **Discussion** - Forum/Discord (1 week)
2. **Formal proposal** - On-chain (3 days)
3. **Voting period** - Token holders vote (5 days)
4. **Timelock** - If passed, queued (2 days)
5. **Execution** - Proposal executed

**Proposal types:**
- Protocol upgrades
- Fee adjustments
- Treasury spending
- Strategy additions
- Connector whitelist

## Community Guidelines

### Code of Conduct

**We expect all community members to:**
- ✅ Be respectful and inclusive
- ✅ Help others learn
- ✅ Give constructive feedback
- ✅ Assume good intentions
- ✅ Follow platform rules

**We do not tolerate:**
- ❌ Harassment or hate speech
- ❌ Spam or shilling
- ❌ Sharing of private information
- ❌ Market manipulation
- ❌ Scams or phishing

**Violations:** Will result in warnings, mutes, or bans depending on severity.

### Financial Advice Disclaimer

**Important:**
- No one in the community (including team) provides financial advice
- DYOR (Do Your Own Research)
- Understand the risks
- Never invest more than you can afford to lose
- DeFi is experimental and risky

### Security

**Protect yourself:**
- Never share private keys
- Verify all contract addresses
- Use official links only
- Be wary of DMs offering "help"
- Team will never DM you first

**If you see suspicious activity:**
- Report in `#security` channel
- Do not engage with scammer
- Warn others if appropriate
