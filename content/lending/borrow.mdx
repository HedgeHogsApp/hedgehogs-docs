Borrowing takes a loan against the collateral you have supplied. The loan and the collateral are both held by your HedgeHog account.

## From Explore

1. Open **Explore** → **Borrow**.
2. Tap the asset you want to borrow. The drawer opens pre-filled with the market's context: **Borrow APR**, **Available liquidity**.
3. The form shows your **health factor** and its projection after the action — so you see the margin you are borrowing into before you confirm.

## What the form shows

- **Health factor after** — projected from your real positions, not a guess.
- **Borrow APR** and available liquidity from the market.
- **E-Mode** — if the market supports it, the account panel lets you change it for tighter, cheaper borrowing against correlated assets.

## The rule of thumb

The nearer your health factor is to 1, the smaller your safety margin. Keep a buffer for volatility.

<Callout tone="warning" title="Liquidation">
  If your health factor drops below 1, lenders can liquidate your collateral. The dashboard flags **Near liquidation** as an alert, and [Auto-repay](/docs/automations/auto-repay) exists precisely to keep you above it.
</Callout>
