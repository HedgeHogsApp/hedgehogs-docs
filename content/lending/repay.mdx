---
title: Repay
description: Pay down your debt — the primary remedy when a health factor is low.
---

Repaying closes part or all of your debt. It is the fastest way to raise a low health factor, and the account panel makes it one action away.

## From the dashboard

1. Open your **Lending** account.
2. Under **Account actions**, press **Repay** — it is the primary action when the account has debt.
3. Choose how much to repay and confirm. The form shows the health factor before and after.

You can also repay with the aTokens you hold — **Repay with aTokens** — which lets you pay down debt using the supplied position itself.

## What repayment does

Your account moves the repayment amount to the lending protocol and reduces your debt. The **Account actions** group keeps Repay first when it matters: an account near liquidation lands on Repay, not on settings.

<Callout tone="info" title="Automate it">
  If keeping the health factor healthy is a job you do not want, [Auto-repay](/docs/automations/auto-repay) repays with earned fees automatically when the health factor drops below a threshold you set.
</Callout>
