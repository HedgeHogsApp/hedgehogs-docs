title: Supply

---

Supplying deposits a token into a lending market (Aave V3, Morpho, or Compound) and starts earning interest on it.

## From Explore

1. Open **Explore** → **Lend**.
2. Tap the asset you want to supply. The drawer opens pre-filled with the market's live context: **Supply APY**, **Available liquidity**, and **LTV / Liquidation threshold** if it can be collateral.
3. Enter the amount and confirm. The first time, the app asks you to **Deploy your HedgeHog account** on that chain and approve the token to it.

The funds move wallet → your account → the protocol. Your account holds the aTokens.

## From the dashboard

If you already have a supplied position, open it from **Lending** → **Supply more**. If the asset is still in your wallet, **Idle** offers **Supply to Aave** — a fast link to the Lend tab, filtered to that asset.

## What the form may state

The supply form shows market facts that are actually measured: the supply APY you will earn, the available liquidity, and the LTV / liquidation threshold when the asset can be collateral. Nothing is estimated.

<Callout tone="info" title="Collateral">
  Supplied assets are collateral by default where the market allows. You can switch **Use as collateral** per asset from the account panel, and **Change E-Mode** for efficient-borrowing modes.
</Callout>
