---
title: Health factor
description: The single number that says how safe your borrowed position is — and what each band means.
---

Your health factor is the ratio of your collateral value to your debt, adjusted by each market's liquidation threshold. It is the number that decides whether you can be liquidated.

## The bands

| Band | Health factor | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Safe** | comfortably above 1 | Your collateral comfortably covers your debt. |
| **Caution** | approaching 1 | Price moves can put you at risk quickly. |
| **Near liquidation** | close to 1 | You can be liquidated if the value of your collateral drops further. |

The dashboard shows the band word under the health factor, and each band's meaning in a tooltip — the tint alone is never the communication.

## Where it is

- The **portfolio banner** — a compact health factor with its band word.
- Each **Lending account** — Debt · Collateral · Health factor.
- Every **borrow or repay form** — the health factor now and after the action.

## What to do near the edge

1. **Repay** — the primary remedy, one action in the account panel.
2. **Supply more collateral** — raise the ratio from the other side.
3. **Turn collateral on** — if an asset is not backing your debt yet, toggle Use as collateral.
4. Or let **Auto-repay** / **Auto-collateralize** handle it: both act when the health factor crosses a threshold you set, using the position's own earnings.
