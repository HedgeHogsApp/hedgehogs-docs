HedgeHogs is non-custodial by construction, and this page says so without dressing it up.

## The model

- Your funds live in **your** HedgeHog account, which **you** own.
- The admin can pause the protocol and set fee rates, but **cannot move user funds**.
- Only you can withdraw. Your withdrawals work even during a pause, and you can revoke the bot instantly.
- Every operation's proceeds return to you.

## Audit status — as it stands

A **Hashlock preliminary audit** (May 2026) of the smart contracts found **1 High, 13 Medium and 22 Low** severity issues, all listed as unresolved in the report.

Those findings were **fixed in code** and covered by regression tests — the tracker in `hedgehogs-contracts/HASHLOCK_FIX.md` marks all 36 resolved — and the report's High (a slippage check that broke cross-decimal swaps) is replaced by a price-aware check in the current source. **However, Hashlock has not yet re-verified the fixes**; no clean / passing audit is published.

<Callout tone="danger" title="Read this plainly">
  There is no "audited and safe" claim here. The contracts are under active development, the last external review is not yet re-verified, and DeFi carries real risk. Start small, understand what you hold, and never risk what you cannot afford to lose.
</Callout>

## What to verify yourself

- The code is open source in [HedgeHogsApp/hedgehogs-contracts](https://github.com/HedgeHogsApp/hedgehogs-contracts).
- The Hashlock report, the fix tracker, and the in-repo "final audit" document (an internal, AI-assisted review — not a third-party audit) are all in the same repo. Read them before you judge.
- Always check the address of anything you interact with by hand against the per-chain deployment output.

## Your controls

- **Revoke the bot** at any time — instant, effective, even during a pause.
- **Withdraw** anything, anytime, owner-only.
- **Pause** any strategy from /strategies.
- **Exit & Uninstall** to close a strategy's positions and sweep funds back to your wallet.
