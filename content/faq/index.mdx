## Getting started

**How do I start?**
Connect a wallet, let the app deploy your [HedgeHog account](/docs/get-started/deploy-account), then make your first move — [Quick start](/docs/get-started/quick-start).

**What is a HedgeHog account?**
Your own smart-contract wallet on-chain. It holds your positions and lets automations act on your behalf. You own it. [Read the model](/docs/how-it-works/proxy-model).

**How much do I need?**
There is no minimum to use the app, but automation is worth its fees at meaningful sizes. Start small and understand what you hold.

**Which chains?**
Base and BNB Smart Chain are live; Ethereum Mainnet is in preparation. [Supported chains](/docs/protocols/supported-chains).

## Custody & safety

**Is HedgeHogs custodial?**
No. Your funds live in your account, which you own. The admin can pause the protocol and set fees but cannot move your funds. [Architecture](/docs/how-it-works/architecture).

**Is it audited?**
A Hashlock preliminary audit found 1 High / 13 Medium / 22 Low; all fixed in code and regression-tested, but not yet re-verified by Hashlock — no clean audit is published. [Security](/docs/security/overview).

**Can the bot take my money?**
No. The bot can only execute the strategies you enable, and its proceeds always return to you. You can revoke it instantly. [Account model](/docs/how-it-works/proxy-model).

## Fees & costs

**What does it cost?**
Supply and LP are **0%**; zaps 0.1%; harvest 0.5%; automations from 0.01% (rebalance) to 1% (harvest/compound). Fees are governance-set. [Full table](/docs/reference/fees).

**Why is deploying free in the app but costs gas?**
Deploying your account is a one-time on-chain deployment — you pay its gas, once, per chain.

## Positions & automation

**How do I see everything I hold?**
[/portfolio](/docs/portfolio/overview) — net worth, value chart, and six panels for liquidity, lending, vaults, strategies, idle and activity.

**What can I automate?**
Harvest, compound, rebalance, repay and collateralize — plus take-profit, stop-loss, sweep, hedge and more. [Automations](/docs/automations/overview).

**What happens if I stop paying attention?**
The strategies keep running while enabled. You can pause, edit or revoke anything at any time — nothing runs without your account.

**Where do I get help?**
Open an issue on [HedgeHogsApp/hedgehogs-docs](https://github.com/HedgeHogsApp/hedgehogs-docs) for docs, or [HedgeHogsApp/hedgehogs-contracts](https://github.com/HedgeHogsApp/hedgehogs-contracts) for the protocol.
