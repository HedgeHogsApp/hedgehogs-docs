title: Fees

Fees are charged per operation, on a **basis** decided by who executes it: you (manual) or the bot (automated). Two contexts exist: `USER` and `BOT`.

<Callout tone="warning" title="Governance-set, not a promise">
  The rates below are the **seeded schedule** from `FeeSchedule.sol`. Every rate is stored in the directory's fee registry and can be re-priced by governance at any time — the app shows the live rate for each operation.
</Callout>

## Manual (you execute)

| Operation | Fee |
|---|---|
| Supply / borrow (lending) | **0%** |
| LP provision | **0%** |
| Zap (single-token → LP) | 0.1% |
| Manual rebalance | 0.01% |
| Harvest (manual) | 0.5% |
| Stop-loss / take-profit (automated close) | 0.5% |

## Automated (the bot executes)

| Operation | Fee |
|---|---|
| Auto-harvest | 1% of rewards |
| Auto-compound | 1% of rewards |
| Auto-repay | 0.05% of rewards |
| Auto-collateralize | 0.05% of rewards |
| Auto-rebalance | 0.01% of position |
| Emergency exit | 1% of position |

## Caps & discounts

- The protocol caps every fee at **4%** (`MAX_FEE_BPS`).
- Referrals: invitee discount up to 5%, referrer share up to 10%, combined capped at 50% of the fee.

## Where fees go

Fees are collected to the protocol collector address on each operation. The rest of every transaction is yours.

Source: `hedgehogs-contracts/src/libraries/FeeSchedule.sol`.
