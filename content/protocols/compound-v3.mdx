<DexIcon protocol="compound" size={24} /> **Compound V3 (Comet)** is a redesigned lending market with a single asset per market and reward claiming built in. Your account supplies, borrows, withdraws and claims through the Compound connector, on Ethereum Mainnet and Base.

## What HedgeHogs reads

Your supplied and borrowed balances, market rates, and claimable rewards.

## What you can do

| Action | Route |
|---|---|
| Supply | Explore → Lend, filtered to Compound markets |
| Borrow | Explore → Borrow |
| Withdraw | Supplied position → **Withdraw** |
| Claim rewards | Position or Explore → claim |

## Fee

Lending operations carry a **0%** protocol fee in the current schedule (see [Fees](/docs/reference/fees)).
