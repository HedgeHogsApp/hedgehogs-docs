<ProtocolHeader protocol="aerodrome" name="Aerodrome" chains={['base']}>
  The liquidity hub on Base. Your account swaps, adds liquidity, stakes in gauges and claims rewards through the Aerodrome connector — including Slipstream, its concentrated-liquidity market.
</ProtocolHeader>

## What HedgeHogs reads

Pool TVL and APR, your LP positions (classic and Slipstream), and gauge/veAERO rewards.

## What you can do

| Action | Route |
|---|---|
| Add liquidity | Explore → Pools, filtered to Aerodrome (or Slipstream) |
| Remove / collect fees | Position → **Remove** / **Collect fees** |
| Stake in gauge | Position → stake / unstake, claim AERO |
| Swap | Explore → Swap |

## Fee

LP provisioning carries a **0%** protocol fee; manual harvests are 0.5%; zaps 0.1% (see [Fees](/docs/reference/fees)).
