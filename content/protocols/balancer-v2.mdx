<DexIcon protocol="balancer" size={24} /> **Balancer V2** runs weighted and stable pools — multi-token pools with custom weights instead of a fixed 50/50 split. Your account joins, exits, swaps and claims through the Balancer connector, on Ethereum Mainnet and Base.

## What HedgeHogs reads

Pool composition, your pool shares, and claimable rewards.

## What you can do

| Action | Route |
|---|---|
| Join a pool | Explore → Pools, filtered to Balancer pools |
| Exit a pool | Position → **Remove** |
| Swap | Explore → Swap |
| Claim rewards | Position → claim |

## Fee

LP provisioning carries a **0%** protocol fee in the current schedule (see [Fees](/docs/reference/fees)).
