<ProtocolHeader protocol="morpho" name="Morpho Blue" chains={['mainnet', 'base']}>
  A permissionless lending market built on isolated collateral pairs. Your account supplies, borrows, repays and manages collateral through the Morpho connector.
</ProtocolHeader>

## What HedgeHogs reads

Your supplied and borrowed balances, collateral, and the market's rates on each Morpho market.

## What you can do

| Action | Route |
|---|---|
| Supply | Explore → Lend, filtered to Morpho markets |
| Borrow | Explore → Borrow |
| Repay | Lending account → **Repay** |
| Withdraw collateral | Lending account → **Withdraw** |

## Fee

Lending operations carry a **0%** protocol fee in the current schedule (see [Fees](/docs/reference/fees)).
