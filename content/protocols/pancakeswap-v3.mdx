<DexIcon protocol="pancakeswap" size={24} /> **PancakeSwap V3** is a Uniswap-V3-shaped concentrated-liquidity market on BNB Smart Chain. Your account mints, manages and closes LP positions, swaps, and stakes for CAKE rewards through the PancakeSwap connectors (V3 + MasterChef). Live on BNB Smart Chain.

## What HedgeHogs reads

Pool TVL and APR, your LP positions and their ranges, and staked/claimable CAKE.

## What you can do

| Action | Route |
|---|---|
| Add liquidity | Explore → Pools, filtered to PancakeSwap (or Zap In) |
| Collect fees / add / remove / close | Position panel |
| Rebalance | Position → **Rebalance** (or Auto-rebalance) |
| CAKE staking | Position → **Stake for CAKE**, claim rewards |

## Fee

LP provisioning carries a **0%** protocol fee; manual harvests are 0.5%; zaps 0.1% (see [Fees](/docs/reference/fees)).
