<DexIcon protocol="curve" size={24} /> **Curve** runs efficient stablecoin and pegged-asset swaps. Your account swaps, adds and removes liquidity, and harvests gauge rewards through the Curve connector, on Ethereum Mainnet, Base and BNB Smart Chain.

## What HedgeHogs reads

Pool composition, your liquidity position, and claimable gauge rewards.

## What you can do

| Action | Route |
|---|---|
| Add liquidity | Explore → Pools, filtered to Curve pools |
| Remove liquidity | Position → **Remove** (single-coin or proportional) |
| Swap | Explore → Swap |
| Harvest rewards | Position → **Harvest** |

## Fee

LP provisioning carries a **0%** protocol fee; manual harvests are 0.5% (see [Fees](/docs/reference/fees)).
