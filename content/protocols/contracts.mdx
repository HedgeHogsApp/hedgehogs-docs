This is the reference surface for developers. The code is the truth; everything here is a pointer to it.

## Core

| Contract | What it is |
|---|---|
| `HH.sol` | Your account — the EIP-1167 clone that routes delegatecall multicalls to whitelisted protocol targets. |
| `HHFactory.sol` | Deploys your account deterministically; tracks owner → account. |
| `HHDirectory.sol` | Protocol config: fee registry, whitelists, pause, collectors, referral. |
| `ProtocolDirectory.sol` | Which protocol addresses are allowed. |
| `PositionReader.sol` | The view surface the app reads (accounts, positions, health factor). |

## Connectors (stateless)

Aave V3 · Uniswap V3 · PancakeSwap V3 (+ MasterChef) · KyberSwap (+ Zap) · Zap V3 · Curve V2/V3 · Balancer V2 · Compound V3 · Morpho Blue · Yearn V3 · Aerodrome (+ Slipstream) · NFT Transfer. Each is a stateless adapter that runs inside your account's context.

## Strategies

- **User-callable:** Aave V3 Lending, Morpho Lending, Compound V3, Curve, Yearn V3, Balancer V2, Aerodrome V2, PancakeSwap V3, Orchestrated Multicall, Aave Migration.
- **Bot-only automations:** Auto Compound, Auto Harvest, Auto Rebalance, Auto Repay, Auto Collateralize, Auto Hedge, Auto Stop-Loss, Auto Take-Profit, Auto Sweep & Deposit, Auto Refinance, Auto Migrate Yearn, Aerodrome Auto-Stake.

## Fee & approval libraries

`FeeSchedule.sol` (the seeded fee table), `FeesLib.sol` (charging), `TransferLib.sol` (token movement between wallet, account and protocols), `RouterGuard.sol` (router allowlist).
