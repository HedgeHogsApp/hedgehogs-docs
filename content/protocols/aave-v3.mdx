<DexIcon protocol="aave" size={24} /> **Aave V3** is the reference lending market. Your account reads its reserves and writes against them through the Aave V3 connector, on Ethereum, Base and BNB Smart Chain.

## What HedgeHogs reads

- **Supply APY** and **borrow APR** per reserve.
- **LTV** and **liquidation threshold** — whether an asset can be collateral and at what rate.
- **Available liquidity** — how much of a reserve is left to supply or borrow.
- Your positions: aToken balances, debt, health factor.

## What you can do

| Action | Route |
|---|---|
| Supply | Explore → Lend, or Idle → Supply to Aave |
| Borrow | Explore → Borrow |
| Repay | Lending account → **Repay** (or **Repay with aTokens**) |
| Withdraw | Supplied position → **Withdraw** |
| Collateral | Account → **Use as collateral** |
| E-Mode | Account → **Change E-Mode** |

## Fee

Supplying and borrowing carry a **0%** protocol fee in the current schedule (governance-set; see [Fees](/docs/reference/fees)).

## Why it works through an account

Aave supports acting on behalf of a user, but HedgeHogs routes everything through your account anyway — so the same account, approvals and automation cover every protocol uniformly, including the ones with no delegation.
