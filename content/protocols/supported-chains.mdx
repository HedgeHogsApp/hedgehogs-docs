HedgeHogs deploys your HedgeHog account on the chains it supports, one per network.

<ChainIcon chain="base" /> **Base** — live. Aave, Morpho, Compound, Uniswap V3, Curve, Balancer, Aerodrome (incl. Slipstream).

<ChainIcon chain="bsc" /> **BNB Smart Chain** — live. Aave, Curve, PancakeSwap V3 (incl. CAKE staking).

<ChainIcon chain="eth" /> **Ethereum Mainnet** — in preparation. Aave, Morpho, Compound, Uniswap V3, Curve, Yearn, Balancer.

<Callout tone="info" title="Your account is per chain">
  Deploying on Base does not deploy on BNB. The dashboard shows, per chain, whether you have an account ready.
</Callout>

A market is only listed where it actually exists — deployments are configured per chain and per protocol. The local development forks (31337 / 31338 / 31339) are tooling, never user chains. The full address map is on the [Contracts](/docs/protocols/contracts) page.
