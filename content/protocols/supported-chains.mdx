HedgeHogs deploys your account on the chains it supports, one per network. Today:

| Chain | Status | Markets |
|---|---|---|
| **Base** | Live | Aave, Morpho, Compound, Uniswap V3, Curve, Balancer, Aerodrome (incl. Slipstream) |
| **BNB Smart Chain** | Live | Aave, Curve, PancakeSwap V3 (incl. CAKE staking) |
| **Ethereum Mainnet** | In preparation | Aave, Morpho, Compound, Uniswap V3, Curve, Yearn, Balancer |

Deployments are configured per chain and per protocol, so a market is only listed where it actually exists. The local development forks (31337 / 31338 / 31339) are tooling, never user chains.

<Callout tone="info" title="Your account is per chain">
  Deploying on Base does not deploy on BNB. The dashboard shows, per chain, whether you have an account ready.
</Callout>

Protocol → chain map: see [Deployments](/docs/protocols/deployments).
