HedgeHogs automation is the loop you see above: earn fees, reinvest or repay, keep the position healthy — with no one watching the screen.

## The mechanism

A **strategy** is a set of rules attached to your specific positions — harvest fees above a floor, compound them back in, rebalance a ranged LP past a band, repay debt when the health factor drops. The bot checks your positions and executes a strategy when its conditions are met.

Two things make that safe:

1. **Execution goes through your account.** Every strategy call runs inside your HedgeHog account, with its rules enforced on-chain — never through a custody-holding intermediary.
2. **The bot's reach is the directory's whitelist.** It can only call strategies that are approved *and* bot-callable, and strategy proceeds always return to you. What the bot can and cannot do is on the [account model](/docs/how-it-works/proxy-model) page.

## Building a strategy

Strategies are built in **/strategies** with the **New automation** wizard — Name → Entities → Routing → Protections → [Authorize] → Review — and need you to **authorize the bot** once. The full walkthrough is [Automations overview](/docs/automations/overview); each primitive has its own page.

## Staying in control

- **Pause / resume** any strategy; the change applies to the next check.
- **Exit & Uninstall** closes every position a strategy manages and sweeps the funds back to your wallet.
- Revoke the bot entirely and nothing can run again — the moment you do.
