The dashboard — **/portfolio** — is your command center. It shows everything your HedgeHog accounts hold, and where your money can go.

## The banner

At the top, one card shows:

- **Net worth** — your supplied value plus LP positions, minus debt.
- The chains your portfolio holds something on, as small logos.
- A **value chart** of your HedgeHog accounts over time (1D · 7D · 30D · 12M). Wallet balances are not tracked yet.
- A compact **health factor** with its band word, covering the protocols it counts.
- **Fast links** under a divider — one button per alert (near liquidation, out of range, rewards claimable, automations paused).

## The panels

Below the banner, six panels order themselves by the value they hold:

| Panel | What it shows | Where a new position comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Liquidity** | Uniswap, Aerodrome, PancakeSwap, Curve and Balancer LP positions | Explore → Pools |
| **Lending** | Supplies and debts on Aave, Morpho and Compound, per account | Explore → Lend / Borrow |
| **Vaults** | Yearn vault deposits | Explore → Vaults |
| **Strategies** | Your automations — repay, harvest, compound | /strategies |
| **Idle** | Tokens sitting in your wallet, with the best supply rate for each | Explore → Lend |
| **Activity** | Your actions, grouped by day | — |

Each panel can be filtered by chain and protocol. **Show all** opens the full list in the side panel.

## Reading a position

Tap any row to open the detail panel: the position's figures (value, earned, APY, range, health factor) and its actions — **Supply**, **Borrow**, **Repay**, **Withdraw**, **Collect fees**, **Add**, **Remove**, **Rebalance**. Actions live in the detail panel, not in the table.

<Callout tone="warning" title="Wallet-held tokens">
  A token still in your wallet (not yet deposited into an account) is not something HedgeHogs can act on yet — that is why Idle offers to move it: **Supply to Aave**, **Add liquidity with Zap In**, or **Swap**.
</Callout>
