Every HedgeHog action routes through your **HedgeHog account** — a smart-contract wallet deployed for you on-chain. It is not a HedgeHogs-controlled wallet; you are its sole owner.

## Why the account exists

Traditional DeFi has you approve each protocol for each token yourself, then sign every transaction. A HedgeHog account gives you:

- **One approval per token** — you approve your account once; it handles protocol approvals as it goes.
- **Automation** — an approved bot can execute the strategies you enable (compound, repay, rebalance…), which requires someone to move *your* positions. That someone is your account, under your rules.
- **Cross-protocol positions** — aTokens, LP positions and debt balances all live in one place you can see and unwind.

## Deploying

1. Do anything that needs an account — supply, borrow, add liquidity — and the app opens the **Deploy your HedgeHog account** panel.
2. Review the estimated network fee. It is a one-time deployment.
3. Confirm the transaction. When it lands, the app shows **HedgeHog account deployed on \<chain\>.**

You deploy **once per chain**. Your account on Ethereum is a different account from your account on Base or BNB.

<Callout tone="info" title="Who controls it">
  You are the owner. Only you can withdraw funds, and you can revoke the bot at any time — even while the protocol is paused. The HedgeHogs admin can pause the protocol or set fee rates, but can never move money out of your account.
</Callout>

## Checking your accounts

The wallet pill in the top bar shows how many of your chains have an account deployed. **Settings → HedgeHog accounts** lists every chain with a Deploy / Deployed state, so you can see at a glance what is ready.
