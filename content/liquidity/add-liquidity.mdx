Adding liquidity puts capital into a concentrated-liquidity pool (Uniswap V3, PancakeSwap, Aerodrome Slipstream, Curve, Balancer) and earns trading fees while the price stays in your range.

## From Explore → Pools

1. Open **Explore** → **Pools**. A market table of DEX pools shows TVL, APR, 24h volume.
2. Tap a pool to open the add-liquidity flow: choose your range, enter amounts, review.
3. Confirm. The first time, the app asks you to **Deploy your HedgeHog account** on that chain.

## With one token — Zap In

A pool needs two tokens, but you can enter with one:

- **Zap In** converts your single token and builds the position in the same transaction, routed through the aggregator connector.
- It costs a **0.1% zap fee** (see [Fees](/docs/reference/fees)).

<Callout tone="info" title="Your position badge">
  In the Pools table, a pool you already hold liquidity in shows a **Your position** badge and a **View your position →** link straight into the dashboard, so you are never guessing where your money is.
</Callout>
