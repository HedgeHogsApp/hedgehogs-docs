A concentrated-liquidity position earns fees only while the market price is inside the range you set. That is the whole job of managing an LP position.

<AutoRebalanceAnimation />

## In range, out of range

- **In range** — the position earns fees.
- **Out of range** — the position earns nothing. The dashboard tints the row, says the band word in the tooltip, and can surface an **Out of range · \<pair\>** alert.

## What you can do

- **Collect fees** — pull what the position has earned so far.
- **Add** — put more into the position, same range.
- **Remove** — take liquidity out.
- **Rebalance** — close the old range and reopen a fresh one around the current price.

<Callout tone="info" title="Rebalance is for automation">
  Since rebalancing is the recurring chore of an LP, it is built as an automation first: **Auto-rebalance** re-centers the range when price drifts past a band you set. You can still rebalance manually — but the automation is the product.
</Callout>

## The mental model

Providing concentrated liquidity is renting out your capital in a price window. The window is the only decision; everything after is keeping it in the right place — and that is exactly what HedgeHogs automates.
