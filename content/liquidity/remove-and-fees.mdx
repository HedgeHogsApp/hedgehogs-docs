Two different exits from an LP position: take the earnings, or take everything out.

## Collect fees

1. Open your **Liquidity** position.
2. Press **Collect fees**. The earned amount — with uncollected fees in the tooltip — moves to your account.

## Remove

Removing pulls liquidity out of the range. Combined with collecting, this is the natural way to reduce a position without closing it.

## Close a position

Closing removes all liquidity and burns the LP NFT. The position disappears from the dashboard ("this position is closed"), and the underlying tokens return to your account.

<Callout tone="warning" title="Order matters when it is a strategy">
  If the position is managed by a strategy, **Exit & Uninstall** in /strategies closes every linked position and sweeps the funds back to your wallet in the correct order — remove, collect, burn — instead of managing each NFT by hand.
</Callout>

## What fees cost

Manual LP operations and harvests carry the fees listed in [Fees](/docs/reference/fees): supply and LP entry are **0%**, harvest is 0.5%, zap 0.1%. Automations are priced separately and often cheaper than doing it manually.
