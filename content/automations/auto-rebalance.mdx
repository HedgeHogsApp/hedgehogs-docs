Auto-rebalance re-centers a concentrated-liquidity position when the market price drifts out of your range. It is the automation-first answer to the LP chore: an out-of-range position earns nothing until someone moves it.

## The flow

1. The strategy watches the price against your position's range.
2. When price drifts past your band, it closes the old range and reopens a fresh one around the current price — collecting the position's value first.
3. The position is back in range, earning fees again.

## Why it is built as an automation

Concentrated liquidity earns fees only inside its range. Checking and re-centering is exactly the job a bot does better than a screen. **Auto-rebalance** runs at **0.01% of position** per rebalance (see [Fees](/docs/reference/fees)).

<Callout tone="info" title="Manual still exists">
  You can rebalance by hand from the position's detail panel. The docs just lead with the automation — that is the product.
</Callout>

## Set it up

In **/strategies → Create automation**, choose **Auto-rebalance**, link the LP position, and set the band — how far price may drift before the range is re-centered.
