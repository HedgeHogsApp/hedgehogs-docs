Auto-harvest collects a position's earned fees and rewards and sends them where you choose — back to your wallet, or converted to a preferred token.

## The flow

1. Your position accrues fees and rewards.
2. The strategy collects them once they cross your floor.
3. It optionally swaps them to your chosen token and moves them out — the position keeps running, the earnings land somewhere useful.

## What it is for

Rewards only compound if they are reinvested, and they only matter if they reach you. Auto-harvest is the "take the profits" side of the loop, and it pairs naturally with Auto-compound (reinvest some, withdraw some).

## Pricing

**Auto-harvest** runs at **1% of rewards** (see [Fees](/docs/reference/fees)).

## Set it up

In **/strategies → Create automation**, choose **Auto-harvest**, link the position, set the floor and the destination, and the bot collects it for you from there.
