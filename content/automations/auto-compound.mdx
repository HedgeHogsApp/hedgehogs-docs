Auto-compound collects a position's fees and re-deposits them into the same position. Left to itself, a position earns interest on the original capital; compounded, it earns interest on the interest.

## The flow

1. Your LP position generates fees.
2. The strategy harvests them and swaps any non-target token to keep the balance right.
3. It adds the proceeds back into the position — the position grows, the next harvest grows with it.

## When it triggers

Auto-compound runs when collected fees cross the floor you set. It is priced at **1% of rewards** (see [Fees](/docs/reference/fees)).

## Set it up

In **/strategies → Create automation**, choose **Auto-compound**, link the LP position it manages, and set the routing split. Everything after is the bot's job.
